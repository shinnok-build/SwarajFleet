# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project
harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Conduct that contributes to a welcoming environment:

- Empathy and kindness toward other people
- Respect of differences of opinion, background, and experience
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes

Conduct we will not tolerate:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies both within project spaces and in public spaces when an
individual is representing this project. Contact the maintainers (Team_Null_Pointer)
to report an issue.
